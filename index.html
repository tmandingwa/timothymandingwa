About

I design and deliver data science, BI, and AI-driven analytics products that turn complex data into actionable business decisions. My work spans fraud detection, agentic and GenAI-powered analytics, and enterprise dashboards used at scale to reduce losses, automate governance, and improve decision quality.

What I’ve built

  • Transaction Fraud Detection Systems

    Hybrid rule + ML pipelines monitoring 20M+ daily transactions, reducing false positives and detecting fraud near real-time.

  • Risk Scoring & Customer Segmentation Models

    Unsupervised learning to cluster customers by risk profile and optimize limits, thresholds, and targeting strategies.

  • Business Intelligence Dashboards

    Power BI + Tableau dashboards for fraud KPIs, trend monitoring, chargebacks, and risk exposure.

  • Agentic Analytics Pipelines

    Agent-style workflows for ingestion, anomaly detection, validation, and governance reporting with minimal manual work.
